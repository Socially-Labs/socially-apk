## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE polished mobile app screenshot, tall portrait 393x852 logical pixels rendered crisply at high resolution, no phone bezel, no montage. Reference image is only a style reference: match its light frosted pearl glass cards, soft powder-blue gradients, Mediterranean mountain harbor and white castle backdrop, deep ink text, elegant editorial serif title, clean sans-serif body, thin white borders, generous 24px rounded corners. Absolutely no navy glass surfaces or dark theme; a small deep-blue primary button is fine. This is the final review page after creation form, not the form itself. Header back chevron, title 'Review your vault', discreet 'Paper vault' badge. Vault name 'Horizon Growth', description 'A patient approach to Solana markets.' Optional selected sailboat banner and vault avatar preview. Summary truthfully shows 'Funding window 24 hours', 'Trading duration 14 days', 'Your seed 100 paper USDC', 'Minimum total 1,000', 'Maximum total 30,000', 'Crew minimum 50 paper USDC'. Caption 'Minimum includes your seed'. Rules: '10% of positive total net round profit at final settlement', 'Funds locked during trading', 'Trading starts after funding closes if minimum is met'. Cost row 'Creation fee 0 · Network cost 0' with Paper context. Artwork note 'Artwork uploads after creation'. Compact readable small typography but do not cram. Bottom action 'Invest 100 paper USDC & create', nearby 'Edit details'. No predicted returns, yield, guaranteed profits, live custody claims, adjustable fees, toggles or extra terms.
