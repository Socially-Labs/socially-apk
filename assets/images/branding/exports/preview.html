What “exact” means here

logo.svg contains real vector paths. Its shapes follow the source; fine texture and antialiased edges are approximations. It is not pixel-identical.

logo-exact.svg embeds the original PNG bytes with no alteration. It preserves the artwork exactly, but the image inside remains raster.

PNG, WebP, and AVIF preserve the original visible pixels. JPEG is flattened onto the existing pale-blue background. The detailed vector is intended for artwork workflows; PNG or WebP is more efficient for mobile display.
